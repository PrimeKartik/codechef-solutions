## 📚 Key Learnings

**1. How Many Maximums**
* Finding local extrema in a sequence does not require the numerical data itself; by analyzing the binary transitions of slopes (a `<` followed by a `>`), you can count the local maximums in exactly $O(N)$ time.

**2. Christmas Candy**
* When evaluating whether an element is smaller than *any* preceding element, tracking a single global "running maximum" completely eliminates the need for $O(N^2)$ nested loops, resulting in a clean $O(N)$ pass.

**3. Just Like Mob!**
* Simulating long, repeating calendar timelines is highly inefficient. Instead, bundle the entire period (normal years + leap years) into one unified "cycle block" and use ceiling division to determine exactly where a specific day lands mathematically in $O(1)$ time.
