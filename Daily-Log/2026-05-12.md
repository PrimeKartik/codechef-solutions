## 📚 Key Learnings

**1. Dazzling AXNODR Challenge**
* Simulating billions of bitwise operations is impossible, but they almost always fall into a periodic cycle; writing out the first few terms by hand to find the modulo 4 pattern unlocks a pure $O(1)$ mathematical solution.

**2. Popular Rice Recipe**
* When dealing with "latest state wins" logic (like overriding previous votes) on small data constraints, treating an array of structs like a manual Hash Map with a linear search/update is highly effective and simple to implement.

**3. Walk**
* Instead of trying to simulate velocity decay step-by-step and guessing a starting number, rearranging the constraint algebraically ($Initial \ge W_i + i$) turns a complex simulation into a single $O(N)$ maximum-finding pass.
